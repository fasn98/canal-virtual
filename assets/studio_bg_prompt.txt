PROMPT PARA GERAÇÃO DE IMAGEM — Cenário de Estúdio Futurista (FutureVerse News)

Wide-angle establishing shot of a futuristic, high-end television news studio,
inspired by the layout of a real broadcast roundtable set. Eye-level camera,
slightly elevated, capturing the full width of the room in 16:9 landscape format.

COMPOSITION:
- 4 sleek, modern chairs arranged in a loose curved arc, facing slightly toward
  the center of the frame, evenly spaced with clear negative space between them —
  leave the chairs empty (no people)
- Camera framing similar to a panel/roundtable news broadcast: wide shot showing
  the full seating arc and the environment behind it
- Reserve the bottom third of the frame as a relatively simple, uncluttered floor
  area with soft ambient lighting — this space will have text overlays added
  later, so avoid busy detail or bright highlights directly in the bottom 20% of
  the frame

ENVIRONMENT:
- Futuristic newsroom/broadcast studio, sleek and modern rather than sci-fi or
  fantastical — polished floors, minimalist architecture, floor-to-ceiling glass
  or paneled walls
- Ambient holographic-style data displays and translucent screens integrated
  subtly into the background walls, showing abstract charts, network graphics,
  and soft glowing UI elements
- Cool color palette dominated by deep navy and charcoal tones, accented with
  cyan/electric-blue lighting (rim lighting on furniture edges, subtle floor
  lighting, glowing panel outlines)
- Soft overhead studio lighting with a few visible light fixtures, avoiding harsh
  shadows
- A large curved or panoramic screen/wall as the backdrop, suggesting a global
  news network control room

STYLE:
- Photorealistic, high production value, comparable to a real flagship TV news
  studio set
- Clean and professional, NOT cluttered with excessive sci-fi glow or neon —
  subtle futuristic accents, not a spaceship
- High resolution, sharp detail, no motion blur
- No text, no logos, no watermarks, no people in the scene

ASPECT RATIO: 16:9 landscape, minimum 1920x1080, ideally higher (2560x1440 or
greater) for clean downscaling.
